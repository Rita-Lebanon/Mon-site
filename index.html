The four seasons

Winter

December – February

The rainy season on the coast, with snow on the mountain peaks. Ski resorts open, such as the Cedars, Faraya and Laqlouq.

Spring

March – May

The weather is mild and flowers bloom, from almond to orange blossom. It is the best time for outings in nature.

Summer

June – August

Hot and humid on the coast, cooler in the mountains. The season for swimming and for festivals such as Baalbeck, Byblos and Beiteddine.

Autumn

September – November

The first rains return and the heat drops. It is the season for harvesting grapes, olives and figs.

Heritage

  • The Phoenicians : They built cities such as Byblos, Tyre and Sidon, and their alphabet is an ancestor of many alphabets.
  • UNESCO sites : Five sites: Baalbek, Byblos, Tyre, Anjar, and the Qadisha Valley with the Cedars of God forest.
  • The cedar : The cedar is the symbol of Lebanon and appears on its flag.
  • Food : Tabbouleh, kibbeh, mezze and olive oil are among the best known.
  • Music and dance : Dabke is a popular group dance, and Lebanese song is rich, including the voice of Fairouz.
  • Crafts : Glassblowing in Sarafand and soap-making in Tripoli.
